Ratings

Rated in stars, recommended by record

After every finished task the customer gives 1 to 5 stars. F2H also checks each delivery against its deadline. A hustler's star average and on-time record decide how high they appear. Great work that arrives late slips down the list, and reliable hustlers rise to the top.

★★★★★Top recommendedShown first. Excellent ratings and almost always on time.
★★★★★RecommendedA solid record. Customers are generally happy.
★★★★★Recommended lessMixed ratings or late deliveries. Shown lower down.
★★★★★Not recommended right nowLow ratings or repeated misses. Shown last until the record improves.

Hustlers with fewer than 3 ratings are labelled New. A customer can also report a missed deadline, which reopens the task and counts against the hustler.
